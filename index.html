Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and streaming with Rapid Iptv.

What is IPTV and how does Rapid Iptv work?

Rapid Iptv delivers television programming and video content directly over your broadband internet connection. Upon checkout, our automated system generates your dedicated M3U playlist link and Xtream Codes credentials within 2 minutes so you can start streaming immediately.

How fast does my internet connection need to be for 4K streaming?

For standard definition, 10 Mbps is adequate. For Full HD 1080p, we recommend at least 25 Mbps. For uncompressed 4K Ultra HD and 60FPS live sports broadcasts, a stable connection of 35–50 Mbps ensures zero buffering.

How long does it take to activate my subscription after payment?

Activation is 100% automated. In less than 2 minutes following checkout, your setup details and credentials will arrive in your email inbox.

Can I use my subscription on multiple devices simultaneously?

You can configure your Rapid Iptv credentials on as many devices as you own. Standard plans support 1 active stream at a time, while multi-room options support multiple concurrent screens.

Which IPTV applications do you recommend?

For Android and Firestick, we recommend TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro. For Apple devices, IPTV Smarters or GSE Smart IPTV work flawlessly. For Smart TVs, IBO Player or Smart IPTV provide the cleanest experience.

What is your refund policy?

We stand behind our server stability with an unconditional 7-day money-back guarantee. If you encounter any technical difficulty our 24/7 support desk cannot resolve, you receive a full refund.
